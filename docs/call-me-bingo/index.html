US and UK-style Bingo

All parts of Call-Me-Bingo supports 75-balls and 90-balls bingo, from displaying results, validating cards and scanning cards.

Configurable Draws

You can configure the draws to be manual (you manuall select the ball that was drawn), automatic (randomly pick the balls for you one at a time) or timer-based (randomly picks at a specified time interval)

Spoken Results

Result from each draw is red outloud. You have the choice to repeat, read each-letter and digit separately, alter the speed of the reading or shut it off completely.

AirPlay Support

Show the results of the draws on the Big Screen with Airplay Screen sharing! You have the option to display the winning pattern on the screen or just the draws and picked numbers.

Winning Pattern Editor

To display on the big screen, or just to validate the winning card, you can create your own winning patterns or select from a list of the most common.

Card Validation

To validate that a card has won you can manually enter the numnbers in the app or use the camera-based scanner to try and scan the card directly in a few seconds!
